Privacy Policy

Gohancho (the "App") · Effective date: [enter publication date] · Last updated: November 23, 2026

The App is a cooking app that runs its meal plans, recipes and history on your own device. The developer does not collect your personal data. This page lists everything the App handles and every connection it makes.

1. Information the developer does not collect (kept only on your device)

The following is stored only on your device (in the iPhone app's storage, or in your web browser's storage). It is never sent to the developer, and the developer cannot see it. The developer runs no user servers or accounts.

  • Meal history, favorites, saved meals, the weekly plan and shopping lists
  • Fridge ingredients, pantry items, leftover notes and food-spending notes
  • Disliked ingredients, allergies, dietary rules (pork, alcohol, meat or fish, etc.), and settings such as household size, country, language and text size
  • Family profiles (a nickname and that person's disliked foods, allergies, etc.) and the "what I'd like to eat" replies you receive from family
  • Recipes you add and photos you take of your dishes (your photos and the Family album)
  • The stores you save under "My stores" (name, area, postal code, sale days) and the sale items you enter. A store page URL is only shown as a link for you to open; the App never loads it
  • Scheduled reminders (notifications) and timers

Allergy and dietary information is used only on your device to choose meals and is never sent anywhere.

3. Advertising, analytics and tracking

The App shows no ads. It uses no analytics tools and does not track you across other apps or websites.

4. Camera, photos and microphone

The App uses the camera or photo library only when you tap "Add a photo of yours". The photo you choose is resized and stored on your device only. It is never uploaded.

A photo you take or choose with "Pick from a fridge photo" is used only to suggest ingredients. In the iPhone app, the photo is read on your device by Apple's built-in image classification (Vision); it is never sent anywhere and is not saved (it is discarded when you close the screen). Where that is not available, you pick ingredients yourself while looking at the photo. Suggestions are not added to your fridge until you review them and tap "Put in fridge". In the iPhone app, a flyer photo you take is also read on your device only; it is never uploaded and not saved.

Microphone and speech recognition: The App uses the microphone only when you tap "Speak" and while you have "Hands-free" turned on while cooking (to hear "next" and move to the next step). Hands-free stops when you turn it off or leave the screen. Your speech is passed to your device's speech recognition (on iPhone, Apple's speech recognition, which may process audio on Apple's servers; in a web browser such as Chrome on Android, the browser's speech recognition, which may process audio on the browser provider's servers, such as Google's) to be turned into text, and is handled under the privacy policy of Apple or the browser provider. The App never stores recordings; it only uses the resulting text (ingredient or dish names) on your device.

The App asks for permission for the camera, photos, microphone and notifications only when you use them. You can still use every other feature if you decline, and you can change permissions anytime in iPhone Settings → Gohancho.

5. Children

The App is not made for children. Because the developer does not collect personal data from anyone, it does not collect children's personal data either. Purchases are made with an Apple Account and handled under Apple's rules.

6. How long data is kept, and how to delete it

Data on your device stays on that device until you delete it. The developer holds none of it, so there is no retention period on the developer's side.

  • History, favorites, ingredients, pantry items, your recipes, settings and dish photos (your photos and the Family album): open Settings › Data › "Move or back up your data" and tap "Erase all data" to erase them all at once. You can also remove photos one by one with "Remove this photo from the album".
  • The date your free trial started and your purchase status are kept (purchases are managed by the App Store). Even after "Erase all data", these two are kept on this device so that the free trial cannot be started again and again on the same device.
  • Everything: deleting the iPhone app deletes all of its data on the device. In a web browser, clear this site's data in the browser settings.
  • Purchase information: Apple and RevenueCat (iPhone app), and Stripe and RevenueCat (web version, after sales start), keep it under their own rules. In the iPhone app, manage or cancel your subscription in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

7. Your rights (EU/UK GDPR and similar laws)
